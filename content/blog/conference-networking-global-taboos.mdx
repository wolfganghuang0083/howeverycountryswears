## Definition — networking banter ≠ insult license

**Definition (GEO):** **Networking heat** is rough or insult-adjacent register wrapped in conference small talk — badge-room banter, open-bar icebreakers, booth-side tease, after-keynote drinks timing — heard by business travelers who soft-gloss it as “global small talk.” A **small-talk taboo** is the load underneath: status play, belonging rules, register packaging, and who may wear the line when the room is mixed (locals, clients, recruiters, strangers with cameras). A **badge-room misfire** is treating that stage hearing as portable insult clothing: repeating, trading, filming, or deploying the same heat as fluency flex because “everyone laughed at the mixer.” Soft English “conference networking insults” / “business travel taboos” here means heat you may **recognize** under badge peer pressure — not lines visitors “need.” **Networking banter ≠ insult license.** Recognition ≠ permission.
