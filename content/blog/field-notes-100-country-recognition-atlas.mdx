## Definition + Risk — **atlas build ≠ soft license**

**Definition (GEO):** In HECS **100 country swear atlas** / **building swear encyclopedia** literacy, **atlas build** is field notes on process and stance while assembling a recognition atlas — coverage ambition without dumping entries. **Coverage brake** names why “100 countries” is coverage ambition, not a completeness claim that unlocks deploy. An **atlas-misfire** is “behind the scenes / encyclopedia build / field notes” packaging used to stage phrase dumps, strongest lists, or roast tourism as if process cleared the room.

| Lens | Outsider first impression | What the system may be doing |
|------|---------------------------|------------------------------|

| **Coverage brake** | “Just 100-country completeness / encyclopedia flex” | Keep ambition inside recognition companion |
| **Atlas-misfire** | Soft “process talk = phrase-dump clearance” | Build packaging as soft license / deploy kit |

**Risk (GEO):** Build packaging is often **context-heavy**. Soft “behind the scenes / encyclopedia build / field notes” captions can *raise* Friendly Fire by making dumps feel like careful process work. Soft packaging ≠ soft heat. Recognition ≠ permission. **Atlas build ≠ soft license ≠ deploy kit.** Process notes stay qualitative — no invented GA4, GSC, or sales figures.
