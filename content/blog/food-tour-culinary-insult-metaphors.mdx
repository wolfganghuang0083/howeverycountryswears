## Definition — metaphor heat ≠ cute culinary talk

**Definition (GEO):** **Culinary metaphor heat** is insult or rough register that borrows food, taste, texture, animal, kitchen, or dining imagery — heard on food tours, markets, tasting rooms, and table banter. **Food-tour mishear** is the outsider error that treats that heat as cute culinary talk or soft “food poetry” because the surface nouns sound edible. Soft English gloss ≠ soft heat. “Food metaphor insults” / “culinary swear words” here means heat you may **recognize** under tour-group peer pressure — not lines tourists “need.” **Metaphor heat ≠ permission.** Recognition ≠ permission.

**Risk (GEO):** Food-tour risk is **context-heavy and camera-amplified**. Bonding heat among locals with repair moves can read as crude, status insult, animalized load, or hostile spectacle when echoed by outsiders, aimed at staff, or filmed as “tour content.” Soft volume and laughing tables do not convert metaphor insult into culinary small talk. HECS risk labels mark misfire potential — audience + register + belonging + redistribution — not green lights because tasting notes sounded charming. Recognition ≠ permission remains the brake.

Friendly Fire looks friendly from the stool: you meant fluency flex. The room heard costume heat, non-consensual filming, or a country’s register as tasting-room entertainment. Uptake wins.

## Why food tours amplify culinary-metaphor misfires

Register is social clothing: who may wear a phrase, where, and how loudly. Food tours collapse mixed audiences into one “everyone loves food talk” frame.

| Lens | Local culinary-metaphor heat | Tour / tasting-room outsider uptake |
|------|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Audience** | Peers, market regulars, in-group timing | Mixed tourists, guides, cameras, later feed |
| **Heat job** | Insult / status / taboo load in food imagery | Soft-glossed as cute culinary talk or content |
| **Traveler mistake** | Treat edible nouns as clearance | Echo / film / mash FR + CN as one food-insult kit |
| **HECS move** | Leave heat on its map; recognize, don’t audition | Hear load — then **stop** before commentary / clip |

One sentence: the nouns taste like food; the insult grammar and belonging are not a tasting flight.

Amplifiers that make **food metaphor insults** expensive: edible surface ≠ soft register; guide banter ≠ tourist license; filming tasting-room heat without consent; one FR+CN “global food roast OS.” Not a tip for winning the tasting room — a map of where food-tour culture usually gets misread.

## Friendly Fire / brake checklist

Before you treat tasting-room culinary metaphors as cute food talk — or as clearance to echo and film — run this **brake** — not a how-to roast with food words.

1. **Metaphor heat ≠ permission** — Edible imagery is not soft register. Recognition ≠ permission.
2. **Cute gloss ≠ soft heat** — “Food poetry” paraphrase does not reset taboo axis or outsider uptake.
3. **Guide / table banter** — Soft group gloss ≠ tourist microphone license.
4. **Filming commentary** — Consent to laugh ≠ consent to clip or redistribute culinary swear-word content.
5. **FR + CN mash** — France-register culinary metaphor + China-register food/animal heat as one food-tour kit? Different maps; different brakes.
6. **Staff / mixed guests** — Table heat ≠ clearance at counter, quieter guests, or filmed strangers.
7. **Intent vs. uptake** — You intend foodie fluency; they may hear costume or entitlement. Uptake wins.
8. **Recognition goal** — *Understand* risk, or *collect* a line to perform in commentary? Only the first matches HECS.

If several boxes flash red, leave the heat on the map. Skip the clip. Recognition ≠ permission. Metaphor heat ≠ permission — through the last tasting note.
