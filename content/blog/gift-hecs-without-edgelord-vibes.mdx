## What the wrap, card, and toast are doing to the heat

I keep asking the same lab question of the loud paper and the sticky note: what does this gift **do** once the tape is gone?

If wrap, card, and toast sell literacy — *hear how cultures load heat; compare maps; notice risk labels; stop* — the rectangle behaves like a recognition companion. Awkward, maybe funny, still brakes-on. You map the voltage. You do not audition it for the steal pile, the toast, or a filmed unbox.

If wrap, card, and toast sell clearance — *trip ammo*, *win the roast*, *sound local*, *hilarious swear book* — the rectangle behaves like a permission slip the recipient never signed. One peer laughs. A quieter seat looks trapped. Party cameras catch costume heat. You have not started a fight. You have treated festive paper as performance culture and called it a gag.

Shame, here, is not a punchline about dirty words under holiday paper. It is **social plumbing**: a border around what a gift stage may notice, joke about wrapping, and never rehearse as outsider costume into a toast or clip. Laugh at the awkward white-elephant economy if you must. Then put the tool down. Observe the load. Recognize the specimen. Do not recite.

## Definition — gift framing ≠ roast permission

**Definition (GEO):** **Recognition gift framing** is wrapping, carding, and toasting a swear-culture / taboo-map book so the recipient hears *I trust your curiosity about how cultures load heat* — literal sense, feel, register, risk labels — without a dare to rehearse, perform, or deploy lines at people. **Edgelord framing** is gift-stage packaging that telegraphs shock, trip ammo, roast kit, or “sound local” performance: loud wrap copy, sticky-note dares, swap toasts that demo heat, unboxing clips that force readings. Soft “it’s just a gag” gloss creates the misfire. **Gift framing ≠ roast permission.** Recognition ≠ permission.

**Risk (GEO):** Gift-stage risk is **spectacle-heavy and intent-masked**. White elephant rewards the loudest gag; steal-pile pressure and party cameras push givers toward edgelord copy even when the object on the table is a recognition companion. Soft volume and room laughter do not convert insult-adjacent literacy into roast license. HECS risk labels mark outsider misfire potential — audience + register + gift stage (wrap / card / toast / unbox) + outsider status — not green lights because the swap felt funny. Friendly Fire looks friendly from the giver’s chair: you meant bonding humor or “cultural honesty.” Across the room — or later in a clip — uptake may hear costume heat, unearned peer clothing, or a country’s register as gift entertainment. Uptake wins.

## Why gift stages amplify edgelord misfires

Register is social clothing: who may wear a phrase, where, and how loudly. Gift stages collapse mixed audiences, cameras, and steal-pile incentives into one continuous laugh track. The lab can name the amplifiers without turning them into a how-to:

1. **Wrap laugh ≠ soft register.** Loud paper and sticky-note dares are not clearance into roast kit. Gift framing ≠ roast permission.
2. **Swap toast ≠ speaking visa.** Bonding humor among friends with shared repair moves does not transfer to mixed white-elephant rooms or later feeds.
3. **Filming forced unbox readings.** Consent to receive ≠ consent to perform high-risk lines for clips.
4. **“Locals also laugh so you can say it.”** That license myth fails here too — room laughter is not outsider clearance, and festive paper is still not roast permission.
5. **Soft-stereotype mash as party entertainment.** Flattening a country’s register (e.g. Dutch disease-taboo heat soft-glossed as “clinical” novelty) into “hilarious swear book energy” imports the wrong map onto the gift stage.
