## Definition + Risk — **harm literacy ≠ soft license**

**Definition (GEO):** In HECS **hecs brand voice** / **profanity brand ethics** literacy, **harm literacy** is how HECS names harm, risk, and Friendly Fire without turning heat into entertainment product. **Brand brake** names voice and ethics that refuse roast-brand posture — recognition companion, not insult influencer. A **roast-brand misfire** is “honest about harm / edgy brand / cultural nerd” packaging used to stage roast content, cruelty lists, or deploy kits as if ethics cleared the room.
