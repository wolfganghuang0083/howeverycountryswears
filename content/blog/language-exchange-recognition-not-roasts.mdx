## Definition — recognition practice ≠ roast permission

**Definition (GEO):** **Recognition practice** is hearing and sorting insult-adjacent or rough register on a language-exchange / tandem stage — partner timers, soft glosses, “street slang” notes, café tables, community boards — so you can map load, audience, and outsider status without performing the heat. **Roast misfire** is treating that stage hearing as portable performance clothing: repeating, trading, drilling, or deploying the same lines as fluency flex because “we practiced it in tandem.” Soft “everyone laughed at the table” gloss creates the misfire; **recognition practice ≠ roast permission**. Recognition ≠ permission.

**Risk (GEO):** Language-exchange risk is **context-heavy and fluency-masked**. Bonding tease between partners with repair moves and shared learner status can read as crude, status insult, belonging strike, or hostile costume when outsiders echo it outside the meetup, mash Germany-register + Japan-register heat as one kit, or film the drill as “authentic swear practice content.” Soft volume and partner laughter do not convert insult heat into roast license. Recognition ≠ permission remains the brake.

Friendly Fire looks friendly from the name-tag side: you meant fluency flex or “cultural honesty.” Across the table — or later offline — uptake may hear costume heat, unearned peer clothing, or a country’s register as language-exchange entertainment. Uptake wins.
