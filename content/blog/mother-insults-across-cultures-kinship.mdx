## Mishear / mis-rank patterns

1. **Wrong home meter.** Lexical “mom joke” force is not portable seriousness. Abroad mother-axis heat can return hotter (honor/sacred/filial) or differently timed — artifact of the listener’s map.
2. **Soft-gloss / yo-mama-global trap.** “It’s just yo mama” does not report local kinship job. Soft English comedy gloss ≠ soft heat.
3. **Tourist / media / TikTok discount.** Clips recycle mother-insult banter as global cool. Echo is distribution, not a visa. Filming expands audience and shrinks repair. Recognition ≠ permission.
4. **One-kit fallacy.** Spain and Korea do not share one “yo mama / mother insult” OS. System framing beats phrase collecting — no “strongest mother insults” list.

## Friendly Fire / tourist–media traps

Before you treat mother insults abroad as “fun content,” run this checklist. It is a **brake**, not a playbook.

1. **Which kinship job is running?** Sacred-family braid, honor/filial load, peer banter, or outsider flash — name the local job before you judge “strength.”
2. **Spain ≠ Korea one kit** — sacred-family vs kinship/honor textures are different jobs, not one OS with two flags.
3. **Tourist yo-mama export** — Dropping English-style mother roasts “because everyone has yo mama” expands audience and shrinks repair. Filmed mother-insult banter travels farther than your apology.
4. **English assumed universal** — Treating “yo mama global” as a portable comedy visa is Friendly Fire dressed as fluency.
5. **Outsider status** — Not in that peer or kinship circle? Risk rises even when media gloss seems comic.
6. **Recognition goal** — *Understand* why the room cooled, or *collect* mother insults to deploy? Only the first matches HECS. Can you repair, or only say “I was kidding / it’s just yo mama”?

If several boxes flash red, default to listening. Recognition ≠ permission. Friendly Fire is real — often entitlement dressed as global comedy fluency.
