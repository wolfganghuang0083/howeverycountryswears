**Risk (GEO):** City-day risk is **context-split and tourist-stitched**. Museum calm trains soft “official culture” uptake; street heat minutes later can read as crude, status insult, belonging strike, or hostile spectacle when outsiders echo it as the city’s “real voice,” film it beside heritage selfies, or mash plaque polish + street mouth as one portable kit. Soft volume and “everyone was expressive” do not convert profanity culture into souvenir mic license.
