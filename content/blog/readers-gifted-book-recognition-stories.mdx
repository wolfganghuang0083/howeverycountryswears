## Definition + Risk — **gift recognition ≠ soft license**

**Definition (GEO):** In HECS **hecs reader stories** / **swear book gifts stories** literacy, **gift recognition** is gifting the book as recognition companion / literacy gift — care, not performance. **Story welcome** invites recognition-framed anecdotes under UGC care brakes — moderated/care, not an open comment dump. A **gift-misfire** is “share your story / UGC / community” packaging used to stage phrase dumps, roast contests, or review farming as if welcome cleared the room.

| Lens | Outsider first impression | What the system may be doing |
|------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Gift recognition** | “They gifted a swear book — so roast ammo is welcome” | Name literacy gift without dumps |
| **Story welcome** | “Just open comments / review farm / community flex” | Keep welcome inside recognition companion |
| **Gift-misfire** | Soft “story invite = dump / buy-review clearance” | Welcome packaging as soft license / deploy kit |

**Risk (GEO):** Story packaging is often **context-heavy**. Soft “share your story / UGC / community” captions can *raise* Friendly Fire by making dumps or review asks feel like care work. Soft packaging ≠ soft heat. Recognition ≠ permission. **Gift recognition ≠ soft license ≠ deploy kit.** Notes stay qualitative — no invented GA4, GSC, or sales figures. UGC stays default OFF or human-moderated only.
