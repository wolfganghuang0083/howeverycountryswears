**Risk (GEO):** Match-day risk is **context-heavy and camera-amplified**. ## Friendly Fire / brake checklist

Before you treat terrace rivalry chants as tourist karaoke — or as clearance to join and film — run this **brake**, not how-to shout sports chant insults.

1. **Crowd ≠ permission** — Volume and scarves are not soft register. Recognition ≠ permission.
2. **Cute gloss ≠ soft heat** — “Just banter” / “passionate football” paraphrase does not reset taboo axis or outsider uptake.
3. **Supporter chorus** — Shared terrace rhythm ≠ tourist microphone license.
4. **Filming / joining tourist chorus** — Consent to watch ≠ consent to clip or redistribute football swear-chant content.
5. **UK + IT mash** — UK-register terrace heat + Italy-register rivalry load as one football kit? Different maps; different brakes.
6. **Rival rows / staff / mixed guests** — Block heat ≠ clearance at opponents, stewards, quieter seats, or filmed strangers.

8. **Recognition goal** — *Understand* risk, or *collect* a line to perform in the chorus? Only the first matches HECS.
