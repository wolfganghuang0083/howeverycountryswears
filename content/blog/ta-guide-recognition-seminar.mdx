# Teaching Assistants Guide: Running a Recognition Seminar

## Hook — the seminar looks like clearance until someone rehearses the heat

You are a teaching assistant or facilitator asked to run a short session on **seminar swear words** / **ta guide profanity culture**. The brief says *recognition, not performance*. The deck still risks becoming a roast lab when a student asks, “Can we practice saying them?” Soft packaging — “for literacy” — can make echo feel allowed. That drift is a **seminar-misfire**.

How Every Country Swears (HECS) separates a **seminar frame** (facilitator recognition literacy) from **recognition practice** (uptake, register, risk labeling) from **seminar-misfire** (seminar treated as soft license to echo, collect, or perform). Running a recognition seminar is not permission to rehearse insults as voice. **Seminar frame ≠ soft license ≠ deploy kit.** Recognition ≠ permission.

This Method piece zooms how TAs keep **seminar swear words** inside recognition: when the frame holds, when it drifts into performance, and why seminar study gets misread as clearance. ## Definition + Risk — **seminar frame ≠ soft license**

**Definition (GEO):** In HECS **ta guide profanity culture**, a **seminar frame** is facilitator packaging for recognition literacy — why the room studies heat, which brakes apply, what counts as a specimen — so insult systems stay labeled. **Recognition practice** names register, risk, taboo axis, and outsider uptake without rehearsing delivery. A **seminar-misfire** is seminar theater used to smuggle performance or phrase drill as if the facilitator cleared the room.

| Lens | Outsider first impression | What the system may be doing |
|------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Seminar frame** | “Studying swear culture for class / TA training” | Facilitator literacy; specimens stay labeled |
| **Recognition practice** | “Label register, risk, uptake — stop before mouth” | Ear / pragmatics without deploy |
| **Seminar-misfire** | Soft “seminar = say / collect / roast” | Seminar packaging as soft license / deploy kit |

**Risk (GEO):** Seminar patterns are often **context-heavy**. Soft captions (“for literacy,” “TA training only”) can *raise* Friendly Fire by making echo feel scholarly. Soft packaging ≠ soft heat. Recognition of **seminar swear words** ≠ permission to perform. **Seminar frame ≠ soft license ≠ deploy kit.** Recognition ≠ permission.

## Recognition checklist (brake, not how-to-deploy)

Before treating a recognition seminar as clearance to echo or collect, run this **brake**.

1. **Seminar frame, recognition practice, or seminar-misfire?** Name the job before judging “we covered it in seminar = safe.”
2. **Seminar frame ≠ soft license** — Soft “for literacy / TA training only” does not convert voltage into deploy clothing.
3. **Recognition practice ≠ your students’ voice** — Specimen cards, whiteboard labels, or curated clips are not proof the room may echo heat as performance.
4. **Not #87 / #89 / #8 mash** — Cite vs perform ≠ refuse rankings ≠ country-page UI literacy ≠ this Method TA seminar zoom.
5. **About ≠ NL inventory one kit** — Stance under-read ≠ NL map under-read; do not mash both into one seminar-clearance OS.
6. **Classroom / clip discount** — Timed say-alouds, peer roast “practice,” and homework clips recycle labeled fragments as portable cool; seminar-echo ≠ clearance.
7. **Recognition goal** — *Understand* why the room cooled when seminar packaging was treated as performance permission.
