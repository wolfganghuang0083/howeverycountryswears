## Definition + Risk — **voice-note hear ≠ soft license**

**Definition (GEO):** In HECS **voice note swear words** literacy, **voice-note hear** is ear/uptake when a clip carries taboo heat — sound/register recognition, not clearance. **Clip performance** names forwarding, posting, or staging a voice note as share/viral act — sending *is* performance. A **clip-misfire** is “just sharing / for education / hear the accent” packaging used to stage viral insult clips as if recognition cleared the room.

| **Clip performance** | “Just forwarding the note” | Sending/posting/staging = perform |

**Risk (GEO):** Clip packaging is often **context-heavy**. Soft captions (“for education,” “hear the accent”) can *raise* Friendly Fire by making viral forwards feel like literacy. Soft packaging ≠ soft heat. Recognition ≠ permission to send. **Voice-note hear ≠ soft license ≠ deploy kit.**
